# Contract Risk Report — TechVendor Ltd (input/sample_contract.txt)

## Risk Rating
**High** — Section 2 (Term and Auto-Renewal) is tagged P1, and Section 4.2 assigns all rights in Customer Data to the Vendor, which matches the P1 "unilateral data assignment" category.

### Critical Summary
The most dangerous clause is Section 4.2, which irrevocably assigns to the Vendor "all right, title, and interest" in Customer Data for "any commercial purposes Vendor deems appropriate," with no duty to notify Customer of specific uses. The most important customer obligation is in Section 2: if non-renewal notice is not delivered at least seven calendar days before term end, the agreement renews automatically and Customer must pay all fees for the next one-year term. Before signing, calendar the non-renewal notice deadline and negotiate a longer notice window, a narrower data license limited to service delivery, and a cap on Customer's liability under Section 5.2.

| Clause type | Excerpt | Risk explanation |

| Auto-renewal without adequate notice | "failure to provide timely notice within this seven-day window shall result in automatic binding renewal and Customer's obligation to pay all fees for the forthcoming term in full" | Non-renewal notice is due at least seven calendar days before term end. Missing that window binds Customer to pay all fees for the next one-year term. |
| Unilateral IP or data assignment to vendor | "Customer irrevocably assigns to Vendor all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data" | Customer irrevocably assigns all rights in Customer Data to Vendor for internal analytics, product improvement, benchmarking, and "any commercial purposes Vendor deems appropriate." Section 4.2 also states Vendor has no obligation to notify Customer of specific uses. |
| Uncapped liability (Customer) | "Customer's liability to Vendor for breach of payment obligations, intellectual property infringement, or unauthorized use of the Platform shall be uncapped and survive termination of this Agreement" | Customer's liability for payment, IP infringement, and unauthorized use has no cap and survives termination. |
| Uncapped liability (Vendor) and punitive damages exclusion | "Vendor's aggregate liability to Customer for any and all claims arising under or related to this Agreement" | Section 5.1 states Vendor's liability "shall not be limited in any manner." Section 5.2 excludes punitive and consequential damages for Vendor only. The liability terms are asymmetric between the parties. |

## Key Obligations

### Customer (Acme Corp)
- §2: Must deliver any written notice of non-renewal no fewer than seven (7) calendar days before the end of the then-current term.
- §2: Acknowledges that failure to give timely notice within the seven-day window results in automatic binding renewal and an obligation to pay all fees for the forthcoming term in full.
- §3.1: Shall pay all fees set forth in the applicable Order Form within thirty (30) days of invoice.
- §4.1: Grants Vendor a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to collect, process, store, use, and sublicense Customer Data.
- §4.2: Irrevocably assigns to Vendor all right, title, and interest in Customer Data, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
- §5.2: Liability to Vendor for breach of payment obligations, intellectual property infringement, or unauthorized use of the Platform is uncapped and survives termination.
- §6: For termination for convenience, must give thirty (30) days' prior written notice, and remains liable for all fees due through the end of the then-current term.
- §7: Any dispute arising out of or relating to the Agreement is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New Castle County, Delaware.

### Vendor (TechVendor Ltd)
- §1: Agrees to provide Customer with access to the Platform as described in the applicable Order Form(s).
- §1: Shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain Platform availability of 99.5% uptime, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
- §2: If giving non-renewal notice, must deliver it no fewer than seven (7) calendar days before the end of the then-current term.
- §3.2: Will endeavor to provide thirty (30) days' advance notice of any subscription fee increase.
- §5.1: Aggregate liability to Customer for claims under or related to the Agreement shall not be limited in any manner.
- §6: For termination for convenience, must give thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
- §7: Any dispute arising out of or relating to the Agreement is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts in New Castle County, Delaware.
