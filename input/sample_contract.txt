SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE VENDOR AGREEMENT

This Software as a Service Vendor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

Acme Corp, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 500 Innovation Drive, Wilmington, DE 19801 ("Customer"),
and
TechVendor Ltd, a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales with its principal place of business at 10 Digital Square, London, EC2A 4NE ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES

Vendor agrees to provide Customer with access to its cloud-based enterprise resource planning platform ("Platform") as described in the applicable Order Form(s), which are incorporated herein by reference. Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain Platform availability of 99.5% uptime, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.

2. TERM AND AUTO-RENEWAL [P1]

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of twelve (12) months ("Initial Term"). Upon expiration of the Initial Term, and each renewal term thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal. Such notice must be delivered no fewer than seven (7) calendar days prior to the end of the then-current term. Customer acknowledges that failure to provide timely notice within this seven-day window shall result in automatic binding renewal and Customer's obligation to pay all fees for the forthcoming term in full.

3. FEES AND PRICE ESCALATION [P2]

3.1 Customer shall pay all fees set forth in the applicable Order Form within thirty (30) days of invoice.

3.2 Price Escalation. Vendor reserves the right to increase subscription fees at the commencement of any renewal term by an amount not to exceed fifteen percent (15%) above the fees in effect during the immediately preceding term. Such increases shall take effect automatically upon renewal and do not require Customer's prior written consent. Vendor will endeavor to provide thirty (30) days' advance notice of any such increase, though failure to provide such notice shall not invalidate the price adjustment or entitle Customer to terminate without penalty.

4. DATA RIGHTS AND ANALYTICS [P2]

4.1 Customer hereby grants Vendor a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to collect, process, store, use, and sublicense all data generated by or uploaded to the Platform by Customer and its users ("Customer Data"), including but not limited to usage patterns, transaction records, and personally identifiable information aggregated or anonymized at Vendor's discretion.

4.2 Customer Data Assignment. To the extent permitted by applicable law, Customer irrevocably assigns to Vendor all right, title, and interest in and to Customer Data for Vendor's internal analytics, product improvement, benchmarking, and any commercial purposes Vendor deems appropriate. Vendor shall have no obligation to notify Customer of specific uses of Customer Data under this Section.

5. LIABILITY [P1]

5.1 Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, Vendor's aggregate liability to Customer for any and all claims arising under or related to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, negligence, strict liability, or otherwise, shall not be limited in any manner. Customer acknowledges this allocation reflects the parties' relative bargaining positions.

5.2 In no event shall Vendor be liable for any indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages; however, Customer's liability to Vendor for breach of payment obligations, intellectual property infringement, or unauthorized use of the Platform shall be uncapped and survive termination of this Agreement.

6. TERMINATION [P3]

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party. In the event of termination by Customer for convenience, Customer shall remain liable for all fees due through the end of the then-current term. Vendor may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Customer breaches any payment obligation and fails to cure such breach within five (5) business days of notice.

7. GOVERNING LAW [P3]

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, United States of America, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

8. GENERAL

This Agreement, together with all Order Forms, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties, except as otherwise provided in Section 3.2.
